Attachment Opportunities

We offer immersive industrial attachments where students gain practical skills, essential life skills, professional development, and workforce readiness.

Why Attach with Us?

Mfano Bora Africa Ltd is dedicated to empowering individuals through mentorship, leadership training, and personalized coaching. We build a highly skilled workforce in the region by giving students hands-on experience in real-world environments.

  • Practical Experience: Work on live projects and solve real business problems.
  • Mentorship: Get paired with industry professionals for guidance and coaching.
  • Workforce Readiness: Develop essential soft skills alongside technical expertise.
  • Networking: Connect with peers and professionals in the ICT and logistics sectors.

Software Development

Work on live projects, learn version control (Git), modern web frameworks, and agile development methodologies alongside senior developers.

Design & Multimedia

Join our creative team to produce digital assets, marketing materials, UI/UX designs, and learn industry-standard design tools.

IT Support & Networking

Gain hands-on experience in hardware maintenance, network configuration, troubleshooting, and user technical support.

Cybersecurity & Defense

Learn threat detection, firewall configuration, system audits, and security vulnerability patching under senior security analysts.

Data Analytics & BI

Work with real database architectures, learn data sanitization, build Power BI and SQL dashboards, and present insights to management.

Digital Marketing & Strategy

Plan and execute SEO campaigns, run content strategy workshops, manage professional social media calendars, and analyze conversion rates.
