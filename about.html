Our Legacy

About Us

Empowering East Africa's tech innovators from humble beginnings to a premier ICT development center.

Company History

Established in 2018 as a modest computer learning room, Mfano Africa ICT Hub was born out of a vision to resolve the tech talent deficit in East Africa. Over the years, we transitioned from basic software testing programs to an all-inclusive multi-campus academic ecosystem. Today, we stand as a premier technology developer, incubating groundbreaking open-source software, producing TVET-certified technicians, and providing high-impact digital learning environments designed for modern workspace integration.

Achievements

Fully accredited by the Technical and Vocational Education and Training Authority (TVETA) and the National Industrial Training Authority (NITA). We have successfully trained and certified over 5,200 software engineers, database admins, and IT security experts. Our proudest metric is our active 92.4% graduate career transition rate, helping young tech minds secure placement in high-growth enterprises and establish successful freelancing systems.

Partnerships

We believe in collaborative industry-led growth. Mfano Africa has built powerful academic and placement channels with leading local tech enterprises, global tech giants, and regional government bodies. These partnerships guarantee that our curriculum remains modern and aligned with standard software development practices, while opening up immediate industrial attachment slots and career placements for our student cohorts. Community Impact

Beyond commercial courses, we operate a dedicated community development division. We hold quarterly free digital literacy bootcamps in underserved counties, organize high school tech-mentorship drives to inspire young girls to join STEM fields, and provide fully-funded educational scholarships to promising students from underprivileged families, driving systemic empowerment across the nation.
